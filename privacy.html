Privacy Policy - Ask Stickman Uploader

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Ask Stickman Uploader is a personal tool used by one person, its developer, to upload the developer's own videos to the developer's own YouTube channel (Ask Stickman). It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the developer signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to the developer's YouTube channel and to read the basic details of that channel, so the app can confirm it is acting on the correct channel. It does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.

How data is used and stored

The access token is stored only on the developer's own computer and is used only to upload the developer's own videos. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data of other people, and it does not send any Google user data to any server or third party.

Google services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project's GitHub repository: github.com/basilisleiva-beep/dark-pc-setups.
