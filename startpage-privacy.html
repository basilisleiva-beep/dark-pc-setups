Privacy policy: The Night Shift Desk startpage

Applies to The Night Shift Desk and Night Shift Desk Lite (the browser extensions and the homepage versions). Last updated 23 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The startpage has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your settings, quick links and tasks are saved only in your browser's local storage on your computer. They are never uploaded. Clearing your browsing data removes them.

What leaves your computer

  • Searches: when you search, your search goes directly to the search engine you chose (for example Google or DuckDuckGo), under that engine's own privacy policy.
  • Links: when you open one of your quick links, your browser visits that site.

Permissions

The extension asks for the favicon permission only to show the small site icons next to your quick links. It does not read your browsing history or the pages you visit.
